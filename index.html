Donelle's Favorite NHL Arenas!

Not really based on anything really, just the first three I could think of.

MY favorite arenas spanning the National Hockey League.

Founded in 1917, the National Hockey League is currently home to 32 teams in 19 US states, 1 in DC, and 5 Canadian provinces.

Donelle's Top 3:

  • Prudential Center, Newark, NJ: Home to 3-time Stanley Cup Champions the New Jersey Devils (and my favorite team, LGD!). The team that everyone loves to hate, and the Home of Instagram Hockey©
  • Centre Bell, Montréal, QC: Home to the 23-time Cup champs, there's a reason why the Montréal Canadiens will always want home ice advantage.
  • SAP Center, San Jose, CA: Home to the June hockey hopefuls, the San Jose Sharks. Their team is young and willing, the Tank is bumping, and #TheFutureIsTeal
